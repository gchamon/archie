# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Archie's owner uses the desktop controls while working in their personal Arch Linux and Hyprland environment. They need to quickly inspect and adjust the system policies that Archie owns, without opening a terminal or navigating a broad settings application.

## Product Purpose

Archie is a personal system configuration and companion guide. Its desktop controls expose live, Archie-owned settings through a focused interface that shares the same sources of truth as the command line. Success means the owner can understand the current system state and make an intentional change with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Personal, dependable, and direct. The interface should feel like a well-maintained tool for its owner: calm and precise, never performative or overly decorative.

## Anti-references

Do not turn Archie into a general desktop-settings replacement, a dashboard, or an ornate status display. Avoid hiding state behind visual-only indicators, excessive decoration, or a dense collection of unrelated controls.

## Design Principles

- Show the live state before the user needs to act.
- Keep desktop controls focused on settings Archie owns.
- Reuse command-backed sources of truth instead of creating parallel state.
- Favor legibility and direct language over decoration.
- Make a system change feel intentional and understandable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use plain-text labels and states. Do not make color the only way to convey a setting's state.
